# Name the Species — Production Classifier

The old Inspector/learning pipeline is retired. The classifier is a built-in silhouette library plus, since 2026-10-13, a compact overlay of silhouettes confirmed after the build (see "Learned silhouettes"), a safety pause and a review page for the wrong answers.

## Production model

The extension ships a compiled silhouette database in `data/species-static.js`.

Source:
- learning export: `2026-10-02T22:54:10.362Z` (source extension v5.10.2), every species in it
- the ten species of the original quiz pool (Canis, Draconis, Equus, Feline, Gekko, Lupus, Mantis, Raptor, Slime, Vulpes): every learned silhouette (150 each) minus 4 mislabeled ones, plus confirmed corrections — 1,499 silhouettes
- 29 further species, thin: the export's Adoption-Center silhouettes (81 in all), the quizzes the game confirmed on 2026-10-10 and the owner's labels of 2026-10-13 — 2 to 24 silhouettes each
- total 1,691 silhouettes in 39 species, ~440 KB

### The quiz pool grew (2026-10-10)

Until then every quiz offered only the ten original species. On 2026-10-10 the wrong-case queue filled to its cap of 250 in one day: the six options now come from at least 39 species (the original ten plus Anura, Avi, Catus, Cebidae, Cetacea, Chiropy, Ericius, Gallus, Gryphus, Haliaeetus, Hyaena, Lepus, Loong, Lotor, Macropus, Mustela, Ovis, Pacos, Phanta, Piscium, Porcus, Psittaco, Rangifer, Rattus, Serpentes, Struthio, Taurus, Testa, Ursa). The 10-species library had nothing for 29 of them, so it either matched an old species within the generous 250-bit threshold (146 cases) or guessed among unknown options (104 cases). None of the 54 cases the game resolved had an original-pool species as its answer.

What exists for the new species is thin: before the confirmed cases were added, the rebuilt library answered 30 of those 54 quizzes correctly with the quiz's own silhouette held out. Expect many wrong answers on new species until more confirmed silhouettes exist: with the owner's labels of 2026-10-13 folded in, the thin species still score only 104/163 leave-one-out over all species (a quiz offers six), against 1,521/1,528 for the well-sampled ones. A species needs on the order of 100 distinct silhouettes; the learned overlay collects them while the helper runs.

The challenge image of a wrong case can still be fetched (`https://app.ovipets.com/img/pet/<eggId>/credit-challenge`, public, CORS `*`, now WebP) and still matches the stored silhouette within about 10 bits unless the egg has been asked a new question since. A retry after a rejected answer repeats the same image and the same six options (measured on 85 eggs with more than one wrong case: 136 of 136 retries); a later day asks a new image. The extension's review page (below) shows that image next to the stored silhouette and the six options.

The file is generated by `scripts/build-species-static.js`; do not edit it by hand.

### Why not a subsample (2026-10-04)

Until 2026-10-04 the library was a 451-silhouette subsample of the same export. Three real quizzes were rejected within a day (Vulpes answered as Canis twice at distance 80 vs 81 and 83 vs 84, a mutated Slime answered as Mantis at 98 vs 112). Two causes:

- Coverage. Leave-one-out nearest neighbour over 10 options was 93.1% on the 451 subsample and 99.2% on the full 1,500. The full set answers the three rejected quizzes without having seen them (Vulpes 40 vs Canis 120, Slime 71 vs Mantis 90, Vulpes 58 vs Canis 153).
- Label noise. The old learner stored three Vulpes under Canis and one Raptor under Gekko. One of those "Canis" silhouettes was the nearest neighbour in both Vulpes mistakes. The builder drops a learned silhouette when none of its 5 nearest neighbours share its label and at least 4 agree on one other species, repeating until stable.

The rebuilt 10-species library scored 1,496/1,499 leave-one-out over all 10 species (a real quiz offers only 6); with the 27 thin species added the original ten score 1,493/1,499 and the thin ones 82/113. That describes this dataset; it is not a guarantee that OviPets can never introduce a new species, pose or rendering change.

### Folding in new wrong cases

Use **Export wrongs** and **Export learned** (review page; the panel has Export wrongs too), then:

```
node scripts/build-species-static.js <ovipets-species-wrongs-….json> [<ovipets-species-learned-….json>] [<ovipets-species-labels-….json>]
```

An owner label is checked against the game's own verdicts on that image before it is used: it must be one of the six options, must not be a species the game rejected for it (on any retry), must not differ from the species the game accepted, and two labels of one image must agree. The build prints every label it refuses (8 of the 196 labels of 2026-10-13: one egg's image labelled Lepus and Vulpes, neither possible).

Every case with a `correctSpecies` (resolved by OviPets on a retry, or labelled by the owner) is appended as a confirmed silhouette at the end of its species (`meta.gameConfirmed` counts them; they are never dropped by the label-noise filter, which only looks at species with at least 20 silhouettes). The build prints how many of those quizzes the library would have answered with the silhouette held out, and the leave-one-out score for well-sampled and thin species. Add `--source <learning-export.json>` to rebuild the learned part from a full export; existing game-confirmed silhouettes are kept. Then run `tests/species-static.test.js` and reload the extension.

## Runtime flow

For each real Name the Species dialog:

1. Open the real egg profile and click the real Turn Egg button.
2. Keep the real Name the Species dialog visible in the egg tab.
3. Read the challenge image silhouette.
4. Compare only the answer options currently offered by OviPets against the built-in library plus the learned silhouettes of those species.
5. Click the selected real option and real OK button.
6. Wait for the real OviPets result.
7. Persist the aggregate `correct` or `wrong` counter; the same answer feeds the safety pause.
8. Only when OviPets explicitly rejects an answer, save one compact wrong-case record for later review. If a retry succeeds, update that same case with the confirmed correct species.
9. When the turn goes through, the game has confirmed the species of that image: its mask is learned (see below).

A rejected choice is remembered only in RAM for that current egg so the immediate retry does not repeat the same answer. It is not written to storage.

## Learned silhouettes

`bg/species-learned.js` keeps one storage key per species (`owehSpeciesLearned_<Species>`, at most 150 masks, near-duplicates within 6 bits skipped, the most redundant dropped when full) and a small index. The answerer reads the keys of the six offered species next to the built-in library. A mask is added when:

- the game accepted an answer (automatic or clicked by hand in an extension-owned egg tab) for a species with fewer than 100 built-in silhouettes, or for any species when that question was first answered wrongly — unless the built-in library already has that outline;
- the owner labels a wrong case on the review page.

A mask is taken back when the owner changes or removes the label, and whenever the game rejects that species for the same image (within 6 bits): a wrong label can not keep producing the same wrong answer. Only color-independent 32x32 masks are stored, no ids or images. **Export learned** on the review page downloads them in the format the builder accepts, so they can be folded into the built-in file.

## Safety pause

The background counts wrong *automatic* answers (a miss on an answer the owner clicked does not count; any accepted answer ends the streak). It pauses after 3 wrong in a row, or when more than 10 were wrong within one hour — both limits are in the panel's Species section, 0 switches one off. On the pause:

- `owehSpeciesGuard.paused` is set; the answerer stops answering (the owner can still answer by hand);
- the tab that saw it stops the friend sweep and the own-Hatchery run if they are active and closes the egg tabs (breeding, database and newborn jobs never meet the quiz and keep running); a quiz that appears while paused does the same;
- while it lasts, the own-Hatchery run (button and auto-start) and **Start full sweep** refuse to start and say why;
- the review page opens and the alert sound plays once (if the Species alert is on).

**Resume answering** (panel or review page) clears the pause and starts a fresh window; the owner starts the sweep or the Hatchery run again. While the library is thin the pause will trip often; that is its purpose.

## Review page

`review/species-review.html` (panel → Species → **Review wrongs**) shows one card per quiz image: the saved cases of one egg whose silhouettes match (within 20 bits) are the same question asked again, so every answer rejected on a retry is struck out. Clicking one of the remaining options labels every case of that image and teaches the mask at once; the badge shows what the helper would answer now. The background refuses a label the game contradicts (rejected species, not an option, image already resolved by the game), and the game's own answer always wins over a label later.

When the picture loaded on the page no longer matches the stored silhouette (more than 40 bits), the egg has been asked a newer question: the missed one can not be shown any more, so the owner labels the picture they see (any species; the nearest known silhouette is preselected) and that newer mask is learned. The missed question itself then stays without an answer. **Remove reviewed** drops the cases that have an answer; learned masks stay.

## Persistent Species data

The production runtime stores the aggregate totals, a capped wrong-only review queue, the learned masks and the pause state:

```json
{
  "owehSpeciesStats": {
    "correct": 0,
    "wrong": 0
  },
  "owehSpeciesWrongCases": [
    {
      "eggId": "…",
      "source": "…/credit-challenge",
      "shape": "<32x32 alpha mask>",
      "options": ["…"],
      "wrongSpecies": "…",
      "method": "shape-match",
      "distance": 0,
      "correctSpecies": "…",
      "ownerLabel": { "species": "…", "shape": "…", "imageChanged": false, "added": true }
    }
  ],
  "owehSpeciesLearned_<Species>": { "examples": ["<32x32 alpha mask>"], "updatedAt": 0 },
  "owehSpeciesLearnedIndex": { "<Species>": 1 },
  "owehSpeciesGuard": { "wrongAt": [0], "streak": 0, "paused": null },
  "owehSpeciesGuardLimits": { "streak": 3, "perHour": 10 }
}
```

The wrong-case queue is deduplicated, capped at 250 records, and contains no image bytes or full quiz trace. `correctSpecies` comes from the game (a retry was accepted) unless the case has an `ownerLabel` without `imageChanged`.

On the first production stats update, legacy learning keys are removed:

- `owehSpeciesMemory`
- `owehSpeciesAnswerIds`
- `owehSpeciesShapes`
- `owehSpeciesInspectorV1`
- `owehSpeciesSeedSeen`

Existing lifetime `correct` and `wrong` totals are preserved during this compaction.

## Data intentionally not stored anymore

The production runtime does not store or collect:

- challenge thumbnails;
- exact-image fingerprints/history;
- question HTML;
- Answer-ID history;
- full correct/wrong mappings for normal questions;
- egg/user IDs for Species learning;
- network traces;
- source-code hints;
- silhouettes of questions nobody confirmed.

The old Inspector, seed learner and MAIN-world Species network tracing stay removed. The review page and the learner that exist now are new and much smaller: they keep confirmed masks only, and nothing about a normal, correctly answered question of a well-covered species.

## Full Sweep performance

Lightweight Full Sweep tabs still block ordinary page images, media and fonts. A higher-priority tab-scoped allow rule permits only the `/credit-challenge` image needed by Name the Species.

The answerer uses the normal challenge image when available and falls back to the guarded Species image fetch only if the direct image cannot be read. No fetched challenge image is saved.

This keeps the real Name the Species step while removing the previous multi-tab storage writes, trace serialization, source inspection and repeated learning work.
